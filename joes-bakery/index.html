Four generations on the East Side

The family business began in 1935, when Sophia and Florentino De La O opened La Oriental Grocery & Bakery on East 9th Street, bringing traditional pan mexicano to the neighborhood. Their son Joe Avila carried it forward, and generations of Austin families have eaten — and worked — here ever since. Today, Joe's Bakery & Coffee Shop at 2305 E. 7th St. is still family-run.

Good to know

Do you take reservations or sit large parties?

We don't take reservations or seat incomplete parties. Max table size is 8; larger groups are seated separately. Gratuity of 18% is included for parties of 5 or more. For groups larger than 6, call us at (512) 472-0017.

When can I place a phone order?

6:00 a.m. – 1:00 p.m., Tuesday – Sunday. Servers are outside to hand off phone orders.

What's the packaging fee?

Paper-goods costs have tripled, so a 3% packaging fee helps a small business keep up.

Do you cater?

We don't cater, but we do offer donations and sponsorships — submit requests at least 3 weeks before your event.

Can I buy a shirt?

Not online yet — call before 1:00 p.m. or email contact@joesbakery.com.
